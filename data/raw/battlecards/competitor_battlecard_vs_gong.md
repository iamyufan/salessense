# Sales Battlecard: vs. Gong
**Updated: Q1 2026 | Target Segments: Mid-Market SaaS, FinTech**

---

## Executive Summary
This battlecard equips reps to position our platform against Gong, the incumbent conversation intelligence leader. We win on **AI-native insights**, **CRM integration depth**, and **deal velocity**—not feature parity.

---

## Why We Win

### 1. **Real-Time AI Scoring, Not Post-Call Analysis**
- **Gong**: Transcribes calls after they end; insights arrive hours later
- **Us**: Generates live deal health scores during calls; reps adjust pitch in-moment
- **Talking Point**: *"Your reps get insights when they can still influence the outcome—not in tomorrow's standup."*

### 2. **Built-In CRM DNA, Not Bolted-On**
- **Gong**: Requires Salesforce/HubSpot connectors; data lives in separate platform
- **Us**: AI reasoning engine trained on real CRM signals (deal stage, competitor mention velocity, contact map changes)
- **Talking Point**: *"We're not a spy camera on your calls—we're a strategic advisor that speaks your CRM's language."*

### 3. **Vertical-Specific Playbooks (Not Generic)**
- **Gong**: One-size-fits-all call framework
- **Us**: FinTech reps see compliance risk signals; Healthcare reps get buyer committee patterns; Logistics reps track procurement timelines
- **Talking Point**: *"Your sales process isn't like SaaS. Neither should your intelligence platform."*

### 4. **Lower Friction Implementation**
- **Gong**: 12–16 week implementation; requires Sales Ops rebuild
- **Us**: Live in 6 weeks; works with your existing CRM and calendar
- **Talking Point**: *"Start closing deals faster in 6 weeks, not watching consultants configure in 4 months."*

## Common Objections & Closes

### Objection: "Gong is the industry standard. Why risk switching?"
**Response:**
> "Gong built the category, and they're great at *recording*. But the market moved. Your team doesn't need a perfect archive of every call—they need to win *this deal* by Friday. We're betting on velocity over completeness. And we're not asking you to rip out Salesforce; we're building on top of it. Can I show you how one of your peer companies at [similar company] went live in 5 weeks and saw 23% faster deal closure in Q1?"

**Close:** Schedule a 30-min guided demo with their top 3 closers on their real deals.

---

### Objection: "Our team is already trained on Gong. There's switching cost."
**Response:**
> "Your team doesn't need to retrain on Gong—they've trained to *ignore it* because the insights came too late. Our platform meets reps where they live (their calendar, CRM, Slack). The switching cost for your *team* is zero. The switching cost for your *vendor relationship* is real, but it's one conversation with Gong's AE. What's *not* negotiable is whether you get actionable insights that move the needle on quota."

**Close:** Offer 90-day free pilot with 2–3 deals per rep tracked side-by-side (ours vs. Gong).

---

### Objection: "We're already using HubSpot Signals. Why add another layer?"
**Response:**
> "Signals are reactive—they flag changes *after* they happen. We're predictive. When a prospect downloads your competitor comparison 48 hours before a call, or when a buyer's title changes on LinkedIn, our AI scores the risk and tells your rep what to say *during* the call. HubSpot doesn't do that. And we integrate so cleanly that your team sees one unified feed, not two."

**Close:** Pull up their last 10 closed-won deals; show where our signals would have flagged escalation opportunities.

## Red Flags (Signs of Fit)
✓ Deal cycles > 60 days (more room for AI to add value)  
✓ Enterprise/Mid-Market with 15+ reps (scale payoff)  
✓ Modern CRM stack (Salesforce, HubSpot, Dynamics)  
✓ 2+ deal reviews per week (governance culture exists)

1. **This week**: 30-min demo (one rep + buyer)
2. **Next week**: Pilot proposal (10 seats, 90 days, 2 deals per rep)

4. **Week 5**: Business review (show early win signals)

## Key Takeaways for Reps
- **Don't bash Gong.** Acknowledge they're good at recording. Pivot to *velocity*.
- **Lead with CRM.** This is unfamiliar territory for Gong; own it.
- **Show, don't tell.** Side-by-side comparison on their real deals closes faster than any slide.
- **Speed is your edge.** 6 weeks vs. 16 weeks isn't a feature—it's a philosophy.
