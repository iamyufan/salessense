## About PulseIQ

PulseIQ surfaces AI-driven deal insights from call transcripts and CRM data — giving sales reps real-time coaching, risk signals, and next-best-action recommendations so they can close faster and forecast with confidence.

**Best fit segments:** SMB, Mid-Market, Enterprise | **Top verticals:** SaaS, FinTech, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Logistics

## Top Objections & How to Handle Them

---

### 1. 💬 "We already use Gong/Chorus for call recording."

**What they really mean:** *"I don't understand what's different about you."*

**Response:**
> "Gong and Chorus are excellent at capturing conversations — but they stop at the transcript. PulseIQ goes further: it cross-references every call with your CRM activity, deal stage, and rep behavior to surface *why* deals stall and *which* ones are at risk right now. Think of it as the intelligence layer on top of your existing recording tool — most customers run both."

**Key differentiators to land:**
- **CRM-native risk scoring** — not just talk-time ratios
- **Deal momentum alerts** — flags deals going cold before your manager asks
- **Works alongside Gong** — not a rip-and-replace

---

### 2. 💬 "Our reps won't use another tool. Adoption is always the problem."

**What they really mean:** *"We've been burned by shelfware before."*

**Response:**
> "That's the number one reason we built PulseIQ directly inside Salesforce and HubSpot — no new tab, no new login. Reps see their next-best action right on the opportunity record. In our last 50 implementations, time-to-first-value was under 14 days, and average rep adoption hit 78% by week 6."

**Evidence to offer:**
- Reference a customer in their vertical (e.g., FinTech: *"A 120-person FinServ team saw 84% weekly active usage by month 2"*)
- Offer a **free 2-week pilot** scoped to one team — low risk, fast proof
- Highlight **manager dashboards** that reinforce usage without policing reps

---

### 3. 💬 "We're not sure the ROI is there for our deal size."

**What they really mean:** *"Help me justify this internally."*

**Response:**
> "Fair question — let's run the math together. If your reps carry a $1.2M quota and close at 22%, moving that to 25% adds roughly $360K per rep annually. PulseIQ typically improves win rates by 3–7 points within the first two quarters. At your team size, what does one extra closed deal per rep per quarter mean?"

**ROI levers to highlight:**
- **Win rate improvement:** avg. +4.2 pts (internal benchmark, 200+ customers)
- **Ramp time reduction:** new reps productive 5–6 weeks faster
- **Forecast accuracy:** reduce pipeline sandbagging by up to 30%

---

### 4. 💬 "We're worried about data privacy — especially with call recordings going to an AI."

**What they really mean:** *"We could get in trouble if this goes wrong."*

**Response:**
> "Completely valid — especially in regulated industries. PulseIQ is SOC 2 Type II certified, HIPAA-eligible, and GDPR-compliant. All audio is processed in-region and never used to train third-party models. We also offer a **zero-retention mode** where transcripts are analyzed and immediately purged. We can loop in your security team early — most complete our vendor assessment in under a week."

**Proof points:**
- Logos in Healthcare and FinTech (reference upon request)
- Standard BAA available for HIPAA-covered entities
- **SSO + role-based access** — you control who sees what

---

### 5. 💬 "We're in a budget freeze right now."

**What they really mean:** *"Help me find internal budget or revisit in 6 months."*

**Response:**
> "Understood — timing matters. Two options: First, we offer a **phased rollout** starting with one team at reduced cost, which lets you demonstrate ROI internally before broader commit. Second, many customers fund PulseIQ out of existing sales tech or L&D budgets since it replaces manual call review and onboarding programs. What does your current tech stack look like? There might be consolidation opportunity."

**Tactics:**
- Offer a **pilot-to-paid structure** with a small initial scope
- Help them build an internal business case — share ROI calculator
- Set a **concrete follow-up date** with a mutual action plan, not a vague "circle back"

## Competitive Landmines to Plant 🌱

When a prospect is also evaluating **Clari, Salesloft, or People.ai**:

| Competitor | Their Gap | Your Counter |
|---|---|---|
| **Clari** | Forecast-only; limited rep-level coaching | PulseIQ does both forecasting *and* in-deal coaching |
| **Salesloft** | Engagement sequencing; weak AI insight depth | PulseIQ surfaces *why* prospects disengage, not just that they did |
| **People.ai** | Activity capture focus; less prescriptive | PulseIQ gives reps the *next action*, not just a log of past actions |

## Qualifying Questions to Reframe Objections

- *"What does your current post-call review process look like?"*
- *"How do you currently identify deals at risk before they're officially flagged?"*
- *"When a new rep joins, how long before they're hitting quota?"*
- *"What would a 5% improvement in win rate mean for your number this year?"*
