# ICP Battlecard: Mid-Market SaaS Sales Leader

## ICP Snapshot
**Title:** VP of Sales, Head of Revenue, Director of Sales  
**Company Size:** 100–1,000 employees  
**Industry:** B2B SaaS  
**Revenue Range:** $20M–$200M ARR  
**Sales Motion:** Primarily outbound + product-led inbound with SDR/AE structure  
**Sales Stack:** Salesforce or HubSpot, Gong/Chorus, Outreach/Salesloft, Slack, BI tools

This ICP leads revenue teams responsible for predictable pipeline generation and efficient deal execution. They are measured heavily on **quarterly pipeline coverage, win rates, sales cycle length, and ramp time for new reps**.

---

## Core Business Objectives
- **Increase win rates and deal velocity**
- **Improve forecast accuracy**
- **Scale sales productivity without proportional headcount growth**
- **Shorten ramp time for new sales reps**
- **Identify and replicate top performer behaviors**

This buyer is under constant pressure from the **CEO and Board to sustain high growth** while maintaining operational efficiency.

---

## Key Pain Points
### 1. Pipeline Visibility Gaps
- Limited visibility into **why deals are stalling**
- Forecasts rely heavily on rep intuition
- CRM data is incomplete or outdated

### 2. Sales Execution Inconsistency
- Top performers consistently win, but **their playbooks are undocumented**
- Managers lack scalable ways to coach reps
- Rep messaging varies widely across the team

### 3. Long Sales Cycles
- Deals drag due to unclear next steps
- Buyers raise late-stage objections that reps fail to handle effectively
- Important signals from sales calls are lost in manual notes

### 4. Coaching Bottlenecks
- Sales managers spend hours reviewing calls
- Coaching is subjective rather than data-driven
- Reps receive feedback too late to influence deals in progress

---

## Buying Triggers
- Missed quarterly revenue targets
- Rapid sales team expansion (new SDR/AE hiring)
- CRM migration or sales tech stack overhaul
- Leadership mandate for **AI adoption in revenue operations**
- Increasing competition leading to declining win rates

---

## Current Tools / Alternatives
Common tools already in the stack include:

- **Conversation intelligence platforms** (e.g., Gong, Chorus)
- **CRM platforms** (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- **Sales engagement tools** (Outreach, Salesloft)
- **Revenue analytics platforms**

However, these tools often **analyze conversations without connecting insights directly to deal outcomes or CRM activity**.

---

## What This ICP Values Most
- **Actionable insights**, not just dashboards
- **AI recommendations tied to real deals**
- **Seamless CRM integration**
- **Automated coaching insights**
- **Fast deployment with minimal admin overhead**

They prefer tools that **fit into existing workflows rather than replacing core systems**.

---

## Messaging That Resonates
Focus messaging on **revenue impact and operational scale**:

- “Turn every sales call into deal-closing intelligence.”
- “Identify why deals stall before they’re lost.”
- “Replicate the behaviors of your top 10% of reps automatically.”
- “Give managers AI-powered coaching insights without manual call reviews.”

---

## Objections You’ll Hear
**“We already use Gong/Chorus.”**  
Position the platform as **going beyond conversation capture to connect call insights with CRM data and deal outcomes**.

**“Our reps won’t adopt another tool.”**  
Highlight **automatic data capture, CRM updates, and minimal workflow disruption**.

**“AI insights sound like another dashboard.”**  
Emphasize **specific deal-level recommendations**, not just analytics.

---

## Qualification Questions
- How do you currently identify **why deals are won or lost**?
- How much time do managers spend **reviewing calls or coaching reps**?
- What’s your **current win rate and average sales cycle**?
- How consistent are your **top-performing reps compared to the rest of the team**?
- How confident are you in your **forecast accuracy today**?

---

## Red Flags (Low Fit)
- Sales team under 5 reps
- Founder-led sales without formal process
- No CRM adoption
- Extremely transactional sales cycles (<1 week)

---

## Expansion Potential
Once landed, expansion opportunities include:
- Sales enablement teams
- Revenue operations analytics
- Customer success call intelligence
- Cross-department revenue insights
